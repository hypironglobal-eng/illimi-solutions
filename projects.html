GiveWell Nigeria — ITN Independent Monitoring & Surveys

Insecticide-Treated Nets Listing, Mapping & Baseline Survey Project

📋 Project Overview

Illimi Solutions Limited is serving as an independent monitoring and survey firm for the GiveWell-funded Insecticide-Treated Net (ITN) distribution programme in Nigeria. The project involves two key field activities: Household Listing & Mapping and Baseline Surveys across targeted Local Government Areas.

CLIENT GiveWell — Nigeria Programme
ACTIVITY TYPE Independent Monitoring & Surveys
STATUS ● Active
LOCATION Nigeria (Multiple LGAs)

The ITN Listing Project involves systematic household enumeration, community mapping by trained Mappers and Listers, and comprehensive baseline surveys. Our field teams are equipped with standardised training materials, data collection tools, and quality assurance protocols to ensure the highest level of accuracy and integrity.
