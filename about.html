About Illimi Solutions Limited

Research. Data. Evidence. Impact.

Who We Are

A trusted partner in research and data-driven development

Illimi Solutions Limited is a professional research and data collection firm headquartered in Abuja, Federal Capital Territory, Nigeria. We specialise in delivering high-quality, evidence-based data and independent monitoring services to clients across the public health, humanitarian, agriculture, and development sectors.

Our name, "Illimi" — derived from the Arabic word for knowledge — reflects our core commitment to generating reliable knowledge that powers decisions, improves programme outcomes, and promotes accountability.

Since our founding, we have worked alongside international development organisations, government ministries, UN agencies, and global health funders to deliver credible, timely, and actionable research across Nigeria and beyond.

Our Expertise

Core competencies that set us apart

📋

Quantitative & Qualitative Research

Household surveys, FGDs, KIIs, structured and semi-structured interviews across diverse communities and populations.

📱

Digital Data Collection

Expertise in ODK, SurveyCTO, KoboToolbox, and other CAPI/mobile data collection platforms for large-scale field surveys.

🌎

Public Health Surveys

ITN coverage surveys, malaria indicator surveys, nutrition assessments, immunisation monitoring, and WASH assessments.

📍

GIS & Spatial Mapping

Household listing, settlement mapping, GPS coordinate capture, and spatial data analysis for targeting and coverage verification.

📊

Data Management & Analysis

Data cleaning, validation, coding, and statistical analysis using SPSS, STATA, R, and Python for robust, reproducible outputs.

👨‍💻

Training & Capacity Development

Enumerator training, field supervisor training, and quality assurance protocols to ensure standardised data collection at scale.
