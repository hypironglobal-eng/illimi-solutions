Welcome to Illimi Solutions

Delivering Reliable Research & Actionable Data

Illimi Solutions Limited is a Abuja-based research and data collection firm committed to providing high-quality, evidence-based insights that drive informed decision-making for government agencies, international development organisations, NGOs, and the private sector.

Founded with a mission to bridge the gap between data and development, we combine rigorous methodological expertise with deep local knowledge across Nigeria and sub-Saharan Africa.

Our team of seasoned researchers, field coordinators, and data specialists brings decades of combined experience in household surveys, independent monitoring, and programme evaluation.

Ongoing Project

Our current flagship engagement

🟢 Active — October 2026

GiveWell Nigeria — ITN Independent Monitoring & Surveys

Insecticide-Treated Nets (ITN) Listing & Baseline Survey Project

Illimi Solutions Limited is currently engaged as an independent monitoring and survey firm for the GiveWell-funded ITN (Insecticide-Treated Net) distribution programme in Nigeria. This project involves comprehensive household listing, community mapping, and baseline surveys to support effective net distribution and independent verification.

Our teams of trained mappers, listers, and field supervisors are deployed across target Local Government Areas to ensure accurate population data, distribution coverage, and programme fidelity.
