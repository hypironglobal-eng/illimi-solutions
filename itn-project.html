Project Introduction

Understanding the ITN Listing & Survey Project

Illimi Solutions Limited has been engaged as the independent monitoring and survey firm for the GiveWell-funded Insecticide-Treated Net (ITN) distribution programme in Nigeria. This project — the ITN Listing Project, October 2026 — is a critical component of GiveWell's strategy to independently verify the coverage, reach, and impact of ITN distribution across targeted communities in Nigeria.

Insecticide-Treated Nets (ITNs) are one of the most cost-effective interventions for preventing malaria, a leading cause of morbidity and mortality in Nigeria — particularly among children under five and pregnant women. GiveWell funds ITN distribution programmes and requires rigorous, independent monitoring to verify that nets reach intended beneficiaries, are used correctly, and achieve the desired public health outcomes.

Illimi Solutions Limited brings together experienced field coordinators, trained enumerators, GIS specialists, and data managers to deliver two integrated field activities: Mappers & Listers (household enumeration and community mapping) and Baseline Surveys (quantitative household data collection on ITN ownership, access, and use).

🌍 Programme Context

The Against Malaria Foundation (AMF) and Malaria Consortium — both supported by GiveWell — distribute millions of ITNs across Nigerian states. Independent monitoring by Illimi Solutions helps verify that distribution records are accurate, nets reach households, and programme data is reliable.

About the ITN Project

Goals, methodology, and field implementation

🎯 Project Objectives

The ITN Listing and Baseline Survey Project has the following key objectives:

  • Conduct systematic household listing and community mapping in target Local Government Areas to create an accurate sampling frame for net distribution and surveys.
  • Enumerate all households within defined enumeration areas, capturing structure counts, household membership, and GPS coordinates.
  • Conduct a rigorous baseline household survey to measure ITN ownership, access, and utilisation rates before distribution, providing the counterfactual benchmark for impact evaluation.
  • Independently verify programme data by cross-checking distribution records against field-level observations and household reports.
  • Generate high-quality, geo-referenced data that supports programme planning, targeting, and post-distribution monitoring (PDM).
  • Train and deploy field teams of mappers, listers, and enumerators according to standardised protocols and quality assurance procedures.

Project Implementation Phases

1
Training

Mappers, Listers, and Baseline Survey teams receive structured training on protocols, tools, and quality standards.

2
Community Mapping

GIS-equipped mappers delineate enumeration areas, identify landmarks, and map settlement boundaries.

3
Household Listing

Listers systematically enumerate all households within enumeration areas, capturing GPS data and dwelling information.

4
Baseline Survey

Trained enumerators administer structured questionnaires to randomly selected households on ITN ownership and use.

5
Data QA & Submission

Supervisors back-check collected data; data managers clean, validate, and submit quality-assured datasets.

✅ Data Quality & Field Standards

Our commitment to rigorous, high-integrity data collection

📋

Standardised Protocols

All field teams work from standardised, pre-tested protocols. No deviations are permitted without written authorisation from the Field Coordinator.

📱

Digital Data Collection

All data is collected electronically using tablets loaded with validated survey forms. No paper forms are used for primary data entry.

🔎

Back-checks & Audits

A minimum of 10% of all completed interviews and listings are subject to back-check by supervisors to verify data quality and interviewer conduct.

🔒

Ethical Standards

All field activities are conducted in full compliance with informed consent requirements, data confidentiality protocols, and research ethics guidelines.
